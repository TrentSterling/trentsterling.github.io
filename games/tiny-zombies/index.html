About the game

The neighborhood has gone to pieces.

Maple Street used to have bigger problems with lawn care. Now the infected are in the yards, your neighbors are trapped at home, and getting everyone to the fallout shelter is your problem.

Tiny Zombies is a top-down voxel shooter about making a mess and getting people out of it. Take the long way around a house, or blast a new doorway. Just remember that the horde can hear you.

01 / THE RESCUE

Nobody gets left on the lawn.

Find the neighbors hiding around the block and escort them home. They follow you, but keeping them alive takes more than pointing at the shelter.

02 / THE DAMAGE

Make your own doorway.

Break fences, punch through walls, and drop heavy wreckage onto the infected. A shortcut can become a trap for whatever follows.

03 / THE DISTRACTION

Give the horde something to chase.

Gunfire and destruction draw a crowd. A rattling soda-can bomb can pull that crowd somewhere more convenient.

04 / THE CHEESE

Yes, that wheel is ammunition.

Shove a giant cheese wheel down the street and let it flatten the opposition. Suburban survival calls for unusual groceries.

Using gear and helping the squad

G selects throwables; left click throws the selected item. F selects a medkit: hold left click to heal yourself or right click to heal an ally. Q returns to your last gun. V toggles dragging a body. The mouse wheel cycles gear; Ctrl + wheel changes zoom.

Latest voice update

The neighborhood has a lot to say.

100 Chatterbox voice lines bring the cast to life, with radio and PA effects, character-matched voices, and new announcer takes. Prefer the earlier sound? Play with the original voices.

Good to know

Use a current desktop Chrome or Edge with hardware acceleration enabled. WebGPU availability depends on your browser, operating system, and graphics hardware.

The first load compiles shaders, so give it a moment. The game is a work in progress, not a finished release.

Contains cartoon blood and voxel gore.

This is the 2026 browser game. Looking for the original Unity experiment? Visit Tiny Zombies (2015).
