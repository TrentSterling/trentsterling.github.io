DuckToy

A little flock for your desktop. Grab, toss, and brush colorful rubber ducks.

Not released yet

One duck. Or a whole flock.

Little rubber ducks that sit right on your desktop, wobble around, and bump into each other.

  • Grab a duck, give it a toss, or brush past it.
  • Add up to 16 ducks, each with its own movement.
  • Shuffle their colors or keep the classic yellow.

Not released yet.

DuckToy is still in development. A public download isn't available yet.
