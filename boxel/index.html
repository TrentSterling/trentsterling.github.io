02 / WHAT

What's a boxel?

Same grid. Six times the surface.

Six faces. Six places to put a color. Our little cube takes that personally.

Minecraft's grass block is secretly a boxel: grass tops, dirt sides, same cubes.

Never touched voxels? A voxel is a 3D pixel. A little cube on a grid. Stack a few thousand and you get a world. Every model on this page is just cubes.

In Boxel, a cube's six faces can each wear their own material. A painted sign on the front. Weathered wood on the sides. A little more character in every block.

One cube, six faces, six materials. That's the whole pitch, distilled into a logo.

Grass on top, dirt on the sides, no split cubes. Per-face overrides are sparse, so a one-color boxel costs exactly what a voxel costs. You only pay for the faces you paint. Import your MagicaVoxel art, then do the face and rect tricks flat voxels can't. Metal, glass, emissive: all on one cube. Voxel++, basically.

03 / FEATURES

Everything in one tool

Model, generate, rig, render

One tool for the whole pipeline, so nothing has to round-trip through three other apps just to end up modeled, generated, rigged, lit, and exported.

Build

Modeless editing

Left-drag builds, right-drag orbits. That's the whole tutorial. Extrude, paint faces, sculpt, mirror, flood-fill. Undo on all of it.

Generate

Procedural + worlds

Terrain, caves, dungeons, mazes, forests, gyroid lattices, knots. One click each. Start with a generated shape or landscape, then make it your own.

Render

Set the mood

Light your scene, choose a sky, and give the windows a glow. Render a clean image or a turntable to show what you made.

Export

Game-engine export

Export GLB for your game engine, OBJ or STL for other tools, or VOX for another voxel workflow. Keep an editable Boxel project alongside it.

Animate

Animation timeline

Keyframes plus procedural rigs. One click drops a jointed humanoid; pick Walk and it walks. This one's doing it right now. Bakes to glTF animation channels.

Script

Agent-drivable

Every edit, camera move and screenshot is a loopback HTTP call. Script it, or point an agent at it, and it can build, look at what it made, and fix its own mistakes.

04 / LEARN

Quick start

Make your first little something.

The app teaches you inside the app. Meet Patch, a practice bot with a few things to fix, and learn by making them right.

  1. Watch it happen. See the tool and the result in your actual workspace.
  2. Take your turn. Place, paint, select, and undo. The coach checks what you made.
  3. Keep your work. Lessons use a temporary document; your project returns when you finish.
