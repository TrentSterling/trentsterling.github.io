The editor

A whole workshop for little worlds.

Start with a cube. Shape it, paint it, and make it your own.

01

Build your shape

Place, erase, extrude and mirror. Start from editable primitives or generate terrain, trees and caves.

02

Find the finish

Paint individual faces. Mix metal, glass and glowing materials, then light the scene and render it.

03

Take it into your game

Export .glb for Unity or Godot, .obj and .stl for other tools, or .vox for another voxel workflow.

Like to tinker? Boxel also has local scripting controls for building, painting, animating and capturing your work.

Why boxels?

Think Minecraft blocks.

Grass on top. Dirt on the sides.
Different faces, same block.

Minecraft's grass block already gets the idea. In Boxel, every little cube can have a different color and material on each of its six faces.

Paint eyes on a character, rings on the end of a log, or books on a shelf. The detail lives on the faces.

Built and face-painted in Boxel.

A little life in your model

Give the parts
some personality.

A head that tilts. An arm that bends. A tiny hello.

Build in parts
Keep the head, hands and other pieces editable. Parent them together in the scene hierarchy.
Put the pivot where it belongs
Rotate a door on its hinge or an arm at its shoulder. Set the joint, then make the pose.
Turn poses into motion
Add keyframes on the timeline. Preview the movement, adjust the timing, and export animation with the model.
