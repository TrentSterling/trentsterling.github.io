Every component on this page draws its colour from one engine. Change the seed and watch all of it move together and stay readable. That is the whole promise: a theme cannot break a component, because no component names a colour.

// foundation

Twelve steps, one job each

The seed supplies hue and chroma character. Lightness comes from a fixed ladder the system owns, which is what makes the guarantee hold for any colour you throw at it.

Lc is APCA contrast. Body text targets 75 and above, captions 60 and above. These numbers are measured live from the rendered colours, not asserted.
