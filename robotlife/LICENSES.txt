# Bundled sound samples

Two unmodified Ogg samples from Kenney's **Impact Sounds** pack are bundled into the offline game. The author lists this pack as Creative Commons CC0: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds . The files' own comments identify Kenney and state that they were generated using GameSynth. They are sampled sound assets, not claimed to be field recordings.

Fetched from the public mirror `GeorgeQLe/assets-sfx-combat`, path `assets/kenney/impact-sounds/Audio/`. Exact Git blob SHA-1 values were verified after transfer:

| File | Bytes | Git blob SHA-1 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| footstep_concrete_000.ogg | 5808 | 163cc56468f1734fe694351e7ff984699afa8b86 |
| impactMetal_light_000.ogg | 7651 | 7d49262cc9f69eb517cf644f61940ace16714f22 |

Footsteps and softer object contacts use the concrete sample. Cookware impacts and robot hits use the metal sample with bounded pitch/gain variation. The one-shot player caps concurrent voices at 12 and collisions are cooldown/distance limited. No audio requests are made after download. Context creation and asset decoding occur following a player gesture.

Pouring and sizzling remain filtered procedural noise; the small music sequencer and some interface/action cues remain synthesized. This is not an entire recorded-audio replacement, nor a perceptual audio-quality certification. All sounds can be muted. Tests verify decoding/playback scheduling, not what a listener hears on every device.

## Sound-effect banks (Kenney CC0)

`assets/sfx/` holds 60 more unmodified Ogg samples from Kenney's CC0 packs Impact Sounds, RPG Audio and Sci-Fi Sounds (https://kenney.nl/assets), fetched from the same public mirror (`GeorgeQLe/assets-sfx-combat`) and verified against Git blob SHA-1 before use; the list is in `assets/sfx/SHA1.md`. `node tools/pack-sfx.mjs` packs them into `src/game/sfx-data.js`. Banks (walk, thud, soft, metal, plate, punch, bell, coin, cloth, pot, slime, click, glass, chop, door) pick a random variation per play: punches use impactPunch, footsteps cycle five concrete takes, thrown or dropped food and trays use soft/plate impacts, coins and the order bell use real recordings, grabbing and tossing use cloth. Sizzle, pour and music stay procedural; there is no CC0 sample for them in these packs. `door`, `chop`, `pot`, `grass` and `wood` are bundled for later hooks and not yet triggered.
